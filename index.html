How it plays

Draw a box around each number.

That's the whole rule. The thinking is in where each box can go.

  1. Drag to draw

    Drag across the grid to draw a rectangle. Tap a box to remove it.

  2. One number per box

    Each box holds exactly one number, and that number is its area. A 6 can be 2×3, 3×2, 1×6 or 6×1.

  3. Fill the grid

    Boxes can't overlap, and every square belongs to one. When the grid is full, you're done.

Support

Questions

How do I restore Remove ads on a new iPhone?

Open Boxed, tap the gear on Home for Settings, then Purchases → Restore purchases. Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Restoring is free.

How do I turn the daily reminder off?

In Boxed, go to Settings → Daily reminder and switch it off. You can change the time there too.

You can also turn off all notifications from Boxed in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Boxed. The reminder is scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server.

Do I need an account? Where is my progress saved?

There's no account and no sign-in. Your progress, streak and settings are saved only on your iPhone, and there's no sync between devices.

Is the daily puzzle the same for everyone?

Yes. Everyone gets the same Daily, Hard and Expert boards each day, and every board has exactly one solution.

How do I change ad tracking or ad privacy choices?

Tracking is off unless you allow it when the app asks. To change it later, go to the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the EEA and the UK, Boxed also shows Settings → Help → Ad privacy choices, where you can review your consent. More in the privacy policy.
